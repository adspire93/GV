📚 *GammaVantage Help Guide*

*Available Features:*

📊 *1. Trade Snapshot*
Get complete 360° view of any F&O instrument
Example: _NIFTY Futures_

💰 *2. Price Lookup*
Query current or historical prices
Examples:
• _What is the live price of NIFTY Futures?_
• _BANKNIFTY 49500 PE price on 15 Oct at 2 PM_

🔍 *3. Market Screeners*
Discover trading opportunities
Examples:
• _Show stocks above 210 ema_
• _RSI oversold stocks_
• _List screeners_ (see all available)

🎯 *4. Trade Simulator*
Calculate "what-if" P&L for hypothetical trades
Examples:
• _What if I bought NIFTY Futures yesterday?_
• _P&L if I shorted 2 lots BANKNIFTY yesterday_
